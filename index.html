Data source & methodology
Discharge source: Open-Meteo Global Flood API / Copernicus GloFAS.
Variable: Daily river discharge in m³/s.
River geometry: OpenStreetMap waterway features where available.
Settlements: Geographic reference points for towns along or near the river corridor.
Processing: Daily, monthly and annual mean summaries are calculated from available records.
Limitations: Modelled discharge is not a substitute for observed river-gauge data. Water temperature, water level and salinity are not supplied by this discharge request.
